# Woodside, CA — Housing Law Eligibility Snapshot (as of Sept 2026)

**HEADLINE CORRECTION vs. prior reports:** Woodside is **NO LONGER non-compliant**. HCD
**certified Woodside's Housing Element on Jan 29, 2025** (state certification announced early
Feb 2025). This directly updates `reports/san-mateo-housing-structural-moats.md`'s listing of
Woodside among "8 non-compliant jurisdictions as of Sept 2024" — that snapshot was accurate for
Sept 2024 but is now ~19 months stale specifically for Woodside. `fact-check-verification.md`'s
caveat that the 8-jurisdiction list is "approximately, not permanently, correct" is validated by
this finding.

## Current Compliance Status (Housing Element)

**COMPLIANT since Jan 29, 2025** (HCD certification), following a multi-year non-compliance
window:
- Woodside was **out of compliance from Jan 31, 2023** (start of 6th Cycle, per Almanac
  reporting) through Jan 29, 2025 — a roughly **24-month non-compliance / Builder's Remedy
  exposure window**.
- Interim milestones: HCD's Oct 14, 2022 letter found Draft 1 did not yet pass muster; Woodside's
  Town Council held contentious public hearings through summer 2024 (July 26, 2024 and Aug 8,
  2024 Almanac coverage document public "outcry" over housing-element site selections); the Town
  Council adopted/certified a programmatic EIR for the Housing Element on **July 23, 2024**; HCD's
  **July 30, 2024** letter found the adopted element met statutory requirements but explicitly
  withheld a "substantial compliance" finding pending completion of required rezones; Woodside
  then completed the rezoning steps HCD required, leading to the **Jan 29, 2025** substantial-
  compliance certification.
- Woodside did not appear on the narrower late-2025 San Mateo County non-compliance list
  (unincorporated County + Half Moon Bay only), consistent with continued compliance through
  Sept 2026. HCD's dashboard itself
  (https://www.hcd.ca.gov/planning-and-community-development/housing-open-data-tools/housing-element-implementation-and-apr-dashboard)
  was checked but does not expose a fetchable per-jurisdiction table — the certification date is
  sourced from Almanac's direct reporting of HCD's certification action, not the dashboard UI.

## SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Status

**SB9 (lot splits) — the mountain lion episode, verified and resolved:**
- **Jan 2022**: Woodside froze all SB9 lot-split applications town-wide, asserting the entire
  town qualified as mountain lion habitat and was therefore exempt from SB9 under the bill's
  species-protection carve-out.
- **Feb 2022**: California Attorney General Rob Bonta sent a formal letter rejecting the claim
  ("An entire town cannot be declared habitat for a protected species... There is no valid basis
  to claim that the entire town of Woodside is a habitat for mountain lions"), backed by a CA
  Dept. of Fish and Wildlife determination that the whole town could not be considered habitat.
- **Feb 7, 2022**: Woodside Town Council reversed course within roughly a week of the AG's
  letter and resumed accepting SB9 lot-split applications.
- **Current status (verified, Sept 2026)**: the exemption claim has remained reversed/dead since
  Feb 2022 — no evidence found of Woodside reviving any town-wide SB9 exemption theory since.
  Woodside currently processes SB9 applications under standard ministerial review.
  **UNCONFIRMED**: whether Woodside has since adopted its own local SB9 objective-standards
  ordinance (as Millbrae and Burlingame have) versus relying on bare state-default SB9 process —
  no such ordinance was surfaced in this search pass, and no local restriction (owner-occupancy,
  historic-district exclusion) was found for Woodside specifically.

**SB35:** No evidence found of Woodside being subject to SB35 streamlined ministerial review in
current search results, and no SB35 project filings found for Woodside. **UNCONFIRMED** against
a primary current-cycle HCD SB35 determination table (not retrieved).

**SB10:** **No evidence found of Woodside adopting a local SB10 resolution/ordinance** mapping
transit-rich or jobs-rich parcels for up to 10 units/parcel — plausible given Woodside's
low-density, non-transit-oriented, large-lot character, but this is a negative/absence-of-
evidence finding, not a directly sourced Town Council statement of non-adoption.
