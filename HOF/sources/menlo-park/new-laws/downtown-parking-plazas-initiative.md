## Key facts (page 1)

- **Official title:** "Downtown Parking Plazas Ordinance" (citizen-sponsored initiative measure)
- **Legal basis:** California Elections Code § 9215 — City Council's Determination of Action on
  a certified citizen initiative petition.
- **Effect if adopted:** Would add **Title 17, Chapter 17.01** to the Menlo Park Municipal Code,
  prohibiting the City from selling, leasing, donating, disposing of, or otherwise conveying any
  City-owned Downtown Parking Lots — for any reason that would affect or diminish availability or
  convenience of parking — without voter approval, with exceptions for maintenance/capital
  improvement work and temporary events.
- **Council's 3 options under §9215:**
  - (A) Adopt the initiative outright as an ordinance (Attachment A)
  - (B) Submit it to the voters at the Nov. 3, 2026 general municipal election, or a special
    election 88-103 days after the call, and direct San Mateo County Elections to conduct it
    (Attachments B/C)
  - (C) Order an Elections Code §9212 report first (up to $164,951 budget, M-Group contract
    amendment) before deciding (Attachment D)
- **Direct housing linkage:** The City's own 2022-2031 Housing Element (certified by CA HCD
  March 20, 2024) includes **Program H4.G** — a plan to develop **at least 345 affordable housing
  units** on a combination of downtown parking lots. The initiative would directly block the land
  disposition needed to execute H4.G without a separate voter-approval step.
