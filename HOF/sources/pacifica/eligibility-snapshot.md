## CRITICAL CORRECTION (flag for downstream reports)

**The Pacifica Quarry / "Coastal Crest Residences" Builder's Remedy project (1,225 units) is
REJECTED, not an "active pipeline" example.** Any report in this project (notably
`reports/san-mateo-housing-structural-moats.md` and `reports/fact-check-verification.md`) that
lists it as active/in-pipeline must be corrected to reflect:
- **Aug 3, 2025:** Pacifica Planning Commission voted **5-0 to REJECT** the project outright,
  ruling that state Builder's Remedy/HAA law does not override the city's 1984 voter-approved
  land-use requirement for the quarry site (a public vote is required before any housing can be
  built there). The developer appealed this denial to the Pacifica City Council.
- **Aug 2026 (article dated Aug 7, 2026):** In a SEPARATE, later procedural track, the Planning
  Commission **unanimously (again) upheld the City's determination that the project is
  inconsistent** with the City's updated 2024 Local Coastal Land Use Plan and the 1984
  voter-approval ordinance — denying the developer's appeal of the City's "Determination of
  Inconsistency." This is not the same vote as the Aug 3, 2025 denial; it is a follow-on ruling
  in the ongoing procedural fight over which version of the coastal plan (1980 vs. 2024) governs
  and whether the project must go to a public vote.
- As of this pass, **no confirmed City Council final vote, no confirmed lawsuit filing, and no
  confirmed HCD/Attorney General enforcement action** specific to this project were found live.
  The project remains stalled/contested, trending toward litigation per a city attorney's own
  stated expectation (see Sources).

## Current Compliance Status

- **NON-COMPLIANT period:** Pacifica was one of ~8 non-compliant San Mateo County jurisdictions
  (with Half Moon Bay, Daly City, San Bruno, Belmont, Atherton, Woodside, and the County) as of
  September 2024 — SMC had the Bay Area's highest jurisdictional non-compliance rate (38% vs.
  19% regional).
- **COMPLIANT since:** Pacifica's 2023-2031 (6th Cycle) Housing Element was adopted by City
  Council **March 24, 2025**, and **certified by HCD as "substantially compliant" effective
  September 19, 2025**. A related rezoning ordinance (General Plan + Zoning Code text/map
  amendments to meet RHNA capacity of ~1,892 units) was adopted by City Council **August 11,
  2025**.
- **Net effect:** Pacifica's Builder's Remedy EXPOSURE WINDOW ran from the start of the 6th
  cycle until Sept 19, 2025. The Quarry preliminary application (filed May 7, 2025) falls inside
  that window and is grandfathered/vested under Builder's Remedy rules even though the City is
  now certified — this is the standard mechanism (Gov. Code §65941.1 preliminary-application
  vesting) and explains why this project keeps proceeding through Builder's Remedy review despite
  current post-certification status.
- **Current progress against RHNA:** per fairhousingelements.org tracker, Pacifica has permitted
  only ~63 of 1,892 required units (~3.3%) as of this pass — well behind pace.

## SB9/35/10 Status

- **SB9 (duplex/urban lot split, ministerial, statewide as of Jan 1, 2022):** No Pacifica-specific
  locally adopted SB9 ordinance was found live in this pass. No evidence found that Pacifica has
  opted into a customized local SB9 objective-standards ordinance; absent one, the **state default
  SB9 provisions apply directly** (this is the default posture for most non-adopting cities).
  **UNCONFIRMED** — needs a direct check of Pacifica Municode Title 9 / a call to Planning to
  confirm no local SB9 ordinance exists.
- **SB35 (streamlined ministerial approval for qualifying affordable projects in
  non-compliant-progress jurisdictions):** No specific Pacifica SB35 application was found live in
  this search pass. Given Pacifica is now HCD-certified (Sept 19, 2025), its SB35 exposure profile
  changes: SB35 eligibility/streamlining criteria are keyed off RHNA progress by income category,
  not Housing Element adoption status alone, so certification does NOT categorically remove SB35
  eligibility if Pacifica is behind on any income-band RHNA progress (and at ~3.3% overall
  permitting, it plausibly still qualifies for at least the below-moderate-income streamlining
  tier). **No confirmed instance found of a developer pursuing the Quarry site or any other
  Pacifica parcel via the SB35 ministerial route instead of/alongside Builder's Remedy** — this
  is a documented gap, not a negative confirmation; flagged as **UNCONFIRMED / needs targeted
  search** (e.g. of SB35 notice-of-intent filings with HCD for San Mateo County).
- **SB10 (local-option upzoning to 10 units near transit/urban infill, voluntary opt-in by city
  council resolution, NOT mandatory):** No evidence found that Pacifica has adopted an SB10
  resolution or mapped any SB10-eligible parcels. Pacifica does not appear in HCD's SB10 adoption
  tracking in the sources surfaced this pass. Status: **not adopted / not mapped**
  (**UNCONFIRMED** at HCD-primary-source level — no direct fetch of an HCD SB10 jurisdiction list
  was completed this pass; treat as a strong negative lead, not a certainty).

## Recent Ballot/Ordinance Changes

- **1984 ordinance/initiative (Quarry rezoning to commercial, imposing voter-approval requirement
  on any future residential use of the quarry site):** This is the "voter-approved land-use
  requirement" cited in the Aug 3, 2025 Planning Commission rejection. Exact measure letter/number
  not confirmed by direct fetch this pass (cityofpacifica.org "Quarry Initiative" page returned
  403 to WebFetch); secondary sources (SF Chronicle, coastsidenews.com) consistently describe it
  as a 1984 voter initiative rather than "Measure D" specifically — **the "Measure D" label from
  prior project research is UNCONFIRMED and may be an error; the correct anchor appears to be the
  1984 Quarry-area initiative/ordinance, not a measure named "D."** Needs primary-source
  confirmation via a live browser session against
  `https://www.cityofpacifica.org/i-want-to/learn-about/quarry-initiative` (403'd to automated
  fetch in this pass).
- **Measure W (November 2016):** A distinct, earlier ballot measure on Rockaway Quarry Residential
  Development was **defeated** by Pacifica voters (per Ballotpedia). This is a separate, prior
  voter rejection event — not to be conflated with the 1984 ordinance or the 2025/2026 Planning
  Commission actions, but supports the pattern that voter approval has historically been sought
  and denied for quarry-site housing before.
- **Local Coastal Land Use Plan (LCLUP) update:** California Coastal Commission voted **8-1 to
  certify Pacifica's updated LCLUP on May 8, 2025**. The updated (2024/2025) LCLUP is the version
  the City is now insisting governs the Quarry site (vs. the developer's position that an older
  1980 LCP version should apply) — this dispute over which coastal-plan vintage applies is
  central to the Aug 2026 Planning Commission ruling.
- **Short-term rental ordinance:** Reportedly adopted ~July 2025; California Coastal Commission
  staff reportedly rejected part of its coastal provisions (per secondary source, unconfirmed by
  primary fetch). Not housing-production-related but surfaced during ordinance research.
- No other 2025-2026 ballot measures specifically on housing approval were identified in this
  pass beyond the above.
