## Recent Changes (2025-2026 ballot measures/ordinances)

- **November 2026 ballot**: San Bruno City Council voted unanimously to place a measure asking voters to **repeal Ordinance 1284**, which currently requires voter approval for any project exceeding three stories or 50 feet in height (with a carve-out for 4-7 story buildings already permitted since 2014 in a 155-acre transit corridor along El Camino Real / San Bruno Ave / San Mateo Ave). This is the most housing-relevant 2026 ballot item — if repealed, it would remove a voter-approval bottleneck for taller infill housing citywide.
- Two other unrelated measures also headed to the Nov 2026 ballot: a business license fee increase and a ban on safe-and-sane fireworks.
- **July 2025**: City Council adopted a resolution launching a fiscal-health initiative tied to land use, housing, and economic development policy changes (broader strategic review, not itself a zoning change).
