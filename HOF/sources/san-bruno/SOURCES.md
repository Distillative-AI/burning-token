## 1. Planning Commission — Agendas & Minutes

- **Name:** City of San Bruno Planning Commission
- **URL (current, primary):** https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/563/Planning-Commission
  (commission overview/landing page) with the actual agenda/minutes archive at
  https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/AgendaCenter/Planning-Commission-8
- **Platform:** **CivicEngage** (confirmed via page title "Agenda Center •
  San Bruno, CA • CivicEngage" on fetch of the AgendaCenter URL). This is
  **not** Granicus, **not** Legistar, and **not** CivicClerk.
- **Archive depth:** Confirmed via fetch — the Planning Commission section of
  the Agenda Center lists year links from **2026 back through 2013**, with a
  "View More" dropdown providing access to further historical records. 2013
  is the earliest year directly observed in this pass; older records (if any)
  were not confirmed.
- **Access notes:** No login wall required to view/download agendas or
  minutes. The site offers an optional "Create a Website Account" feature
  ("Manage notification subscriptions, save form progress and more") but this
  is **not** required for public agenda/minutes access. No CAPTCHA
  encountered in search/fetch. The Planning Commission meets the third
  Tuesday of the month at 7:00 p.m., currently at the San Bruno Recreation and
  Aquatics Center (RAC), Community Room, 251 City Park Way, and is also
  streamed live on Zoom (per search snippet — meeting location/format not
  independently re-verified beyond the search result).
- **Est. pull effort:** **Low.** CivicEngage's Agenda Center presents a
  single per-body page with a year selector; this is a well-structured,
  shallow-navigation archive compared to multi-hop Granicus/eCode360
  structures seen in other SMC jurisdictions.

## 2. Transportation-Equivalent Commission (Complete Streets Committee)

- **Name:** San Bruno does **not** have a body called "Transportation
  Commission." As of **June 10, 2025**, the City Council merged the
  **Traffic Safety and Parking Committee (TSPC)** with the **Bicycle and
  Pedestrian Advisory Committee (BPAC)** into a single new body, the
  **Complete Streets Committee (CSC)** — confirmed via search snippets
  citing the June 2025 Council action and a "NOTICE OF COMMITTEE
  DISSOLVEMENT" agenda-center document
  (https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_07022025-2380).
  The CSC acts in an advisory capacity to City Council on multi-modal
  (bicycle, vehicle, pedestrian) transportation matters and reviews Bicycle
  and Pedestrian Plans, Safe Routes to School Plans, Street Master Plans, and
  transportation-related Capital Improvement Projects, with a stated policy
  focus on implementing the Complete Streets Act of 2008 (AB 1358) and
  General Plan Policy T-A (per search snippet from the Complete Streets
  Committee city page).
- **URL (current, primary):** https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/2237/Complete-Streets-Committee
  (commission landing page). Legacy/predecessor pages, both likely still
  live as historical record:
  - https://sanbruno.ca.gov/594/Traffic-Safety-Parking (former TSPC)
  - Bicycle and Pedestrian Advisory Committee agenda example (predecessor
    body):
    https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_04232025-2335
- **Archive/agenda center path:** Not independently confirmed by direct
  fetch in this pass — by analogy with the Planning Commission's
  `/AgendaCenter/Planning-Commission-8` pattern, a body-specific
  `/AgendaCenter/Complete-Streets-Committee-<id>` page almost certainly
  exists; **treat as unverified-but-plausible** and confirm the exact
  numeric/slug id live rather than guessing it. A prior-generation
  `/AgendaCenter/Traffic-Safety-Parking-14` -style URL surfaced in search
  navigation context but was **not directly fetched/confirmed** in this pass.
- **Meeting details:** Meetings held at San Bruno City Hall, Conference Room
  115, 567 El Camino Real, San Bruno, CA 94066, and streamed live on Zoom
  (per search snippet).
- **Access notes:** Same CivicEngage platform and access posture as Planning
  Commission — no login wall observed for agenda/minutes access.
- **Est. pull effort:** **Low–Medium.** The committee itself is very
  new (post-June 2025 merger), so continuity of record requires
  cross-referencing the CSC's own archive against the two predecessor
  committees' (TSPC, BPAC) historical agendas for anything before mid-2025.

## 3. Recently Adopted Ordinances / Housing-Related Municipal Code Updates

- **Name:** City of San Bruno Municipal Code (housing/zoning-relevant titles)
- **URL (official code host):** https://ecode360.com/SA5001 — **General
  Code's eCode360 platform**, confirmed via multiple direct eCode360 search
  hits under the `SA5001` code id (e.g. `https://ecode360.com/SA5001/index?letter=D`,
  `https://ecode360.com/SA5001/laws/LF2504256.pdf` for Ordinance No. 1966).
  This is eCode360, **not Municode**. The original codification is the "San
  Bruno Municipal Code Revised and Republished December 1998," authorized by
  Ordinance No. 1604 (adopted January 11, 1999) — per search snippet, not
  independently re-fetched from the eCode360 front matter in this pass.
  - Search evidence indicates the eCode360 text is **current through
    Ordinance No. 1968, adopted November 12, 2025** (per search snippet
    summarizing eCode360's own "current through" statement) — treat as
    unverified-but-plausible pending a live confirmation of the actual
    "current through" banner on the eCode360 homepage.
  - Relevant zoning sub-pages surfaced by search (titles confirmed via
    search result snippets, not independently re-fetched for full content):
    - "DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS FOR THE R-1 AND R-2 ZONING DISTRICTS" —
      https://ecode360.com/44260639
    - "MIXED-USE ZONING DISTRICTS" (Title/Chapter overview) —
      https://ecode360.com/44261175 and Chapter 12.280 —
      https://ecode360.com/44261149
    - "ZONING ADMINISTRATION" — https://ecode360.com/44258904
    - "TITLE, PURPOSE, ETC." (zoning title front matter) —
      https://ecode360.com/44258878
    - "CODE ADOPTION" — https://ecode360.com/44247506
    - "AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROGRAM" — https://ecode360.com/44260766
  - Zoning districts referenced in secondary/search-snippet sources include
    R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4, C-N, M-1, CBD (Central Business District), TOD-S
    (Transit-Oriented Development – Station Office), TOD-1, TOD-2, MX-R,
    plus P-D and Bayhill specific-plan districts — **not independently
    verified against the primary eCode360 text in full** in this pass.
  - A specific recent zoning action found: San Bruno rescinded and replaced
    Chapter 12.250 (Density Bonuses) to implement the city's density bonus
    regulations under Housing Element Program 5-B (per search snippet citing
    a zoning code amendments summary document,
    https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1753/Zoning-Code-Amendments-Summary-Table-PDF).
    This document's exact adoption date/ordinance number was **not
    confirmed** in this pass.
- **Housing Element compliance status (verified, current as of this
  research pass — 2026-09-05):** San Bruno was one of **8 San Mateo County
  jurisdictions** (alongside Pacifica, Half Moon Bay, Daly City, Belmont,
  Atherton, Woodside, and San Mateo County itself) reported as **out of
  Housing Element compliance as of September 2024** (per
  https://hlcsmc.org/2024/09/27/housing-element-updates/), consistent with
  the companion San Mateo County structural-moats report's framing and thus
  **Builder's Remedy exposed** during that window. **This status has since
  changed:** San Bruno adopted an **amended 2023-2031 Housing Element on
  August 27, 2024**, and on **October 21, 2024**, the California Department
  of Housing and Community Development (HCD) found the amended element to be
  in **substantial compliance** with state law — confirmed via direct fetch
  of the city's own Housing Element page
  (https://www.sanbruno.ca.gov/568/Housing-Element / mirrored at
  https://sanbruno.ca.gov/568/2023-2031-Housing-Element-Update), which
  states this determination directly. **Current status: in substantial
  compliance since October 21, 2024** — no longer in the non-compliant
  cohort as of this research pass, though it was during the Sept. 2024
  snapshot the companion report used.
  - Per the same city page, San Bruno's Housing Element commits to further
    program actions through 2026, including adding sites for roughly 500
    more units to the Sites Inventory (via Crestmoor, Tanforan, and other
    developments) and creating objective design standards for residential
    development in 2026 — these are **forward commitments, not yet
    completed actions**, per the source.
  - RHNA allocation context (search snippet, not independently re-verified
    against the primary Housing Element PDF): San Bruno is required to
    provide zoned capacity for at least 3,165 residential units, with the
    Housing Element identifying capacity for up to 3,640 units for the
    2023–2031 cycle.
- **Builder's Remedy project applications:** **No specific Builder's Remedy
  project application for San Bruno was located via search or fetch in this
  pass.** The city's own Housing Element page makes no mention of any
  Builder's Remedy applications received. Given San Bruno's non-compliance
  window ran only from an unconfirmed start date through October 21, 2024
  (a comparatively short/late window relative to some other SMC
  jurisdictions), it is plausible fewer or no Builder's Remedy pre-applications
  were filed against San Bruno specifically — but this is an **inference,
  not a confirmed fact**. A live search of San Bruno's Planning Division
  project-application tracker or direct outreach to city planning staff
  would be needed to confirm definitively. Flag as **unconfirmed**.
- **Access notes:** eCode360 pages returned normal search-indexed content
  (search snippets successfully summarized eCode360 page content), unlike
  the HTTP 403 bot-gating seen on some other SMC jurisdictions' eCode360
  instances in prior research passes — however, a **direct WebFetch of an
  eCode360 URL was not attempted in this pass** (only WebSearch snippets were
  used), so live-browser behavior (potential Cloudflare/bot-challenge
  interstitials) is **not independently confirmed** and should be verified
  live. A `https://ecode360.com/user/login/SA5001?permissionError=true` URL
  surfaced in search results, suggesting **some** eCode360 content (likely
  administrative/internal pages, not general code text) is login-gated —
  general public code browsing has not historically required login on
  eCode360 instances and no evidence here contradicts that, but treat any
  page that redirects to `/user/login/` as gated and note it rather than
  assuming free access. sanbruno.ca.gov CivicEngage pages (Agenda Center,
  Housing Element, DocumentCenter PDFs) had no access barrier encountered.
- **Est. pull effort:** **Medium.** eCode360 content is fragmented across
  many individually-indexed section URLs rather than one canonical
  "Title X" landing page in the search results gathered; a live browser
  session starting from https://ecode360.com/SA5001 and using its own
  table-of-contents navigation will likely be more efficient than following
  the scattered deep-link URLs found via search. The Housing Element
  compliance status itself was straightforward to confirm from a single
  primary source page.

## 4. Login Walls / CAPTCHA / Pagination Notes for a Human+Browser Session

- **sanbruno.ca.gov (CivicEngage):** No login wall or CAPTCHA encountered on
  Agenda Center, commission landing pages, Housing Element page, or
  DocumentCenter PDF links. An optional "Create a Website Account" flow
  exists for notification subscriptions but is not required for public
  document access. Year-based navigation on the Agenda Center (2013–2026 for
  Planning Commission) is straightforward — no pagination issue observed
  beyond selecting a year from a dropdown/list.
- **sanbrunocable.com:** A secondary/mirror domain (CityNet Services) also
  surfaces calendar and Housing Element content
  (e.g. `sanbrunocable.com/568/2023-2031-Housing-Element`,
  `sanbrunocable.com/Calendar.aspx?EID=...`) — appears to be a
  parallel/legacy or cable-access-affiliated mirror of city content rather
  than the primary site. Treat `sanbruno.ca.gov` as canonical and this as a
  secondary confirmation source only; **not independently verified** as to
  whether it is fully in sync with the primary site at all times.
- **ecode360.com (San Bruno Municipal Code):** Not directly fetched (browser
  behavior) in this pass — only WebSearch snippets were used, which
  successfully returned content, suggesting no hard bot-block at the search-
  crawler level. However, a `/user/login/SA5001?permissionError=true` URL was
  observed in results, indicating **some** eCode360 sub-pages or actions do
  require login — a live session should watch for any page that redirects to
  this login path and treat that specific content as gated, while general
  code-text browsing is expected to remain open per eCode360's typical public
  posture. **Not fully verified either way in this pass — flag for live
  confirmation.**
- **No CAPTCHA was encountered** on any sanbruno.ca.gov or ecode360.com
  search/fetch result in this research pass.
