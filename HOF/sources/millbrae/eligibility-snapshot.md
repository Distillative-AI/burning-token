# Millbrae, CA — Housing Law Eligibility Snapshot (as of Sept 2026)

## Current Compliance Status (Housing Element)

**COMPLIANT.** HCD found Millbrae's 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Housing Element in substantial
compliance with State Housing Element Law on **March 1, 2024**, per HCD's letter to the City.
The Millbrae City Council adopted the 6th Cycle update on **Feb 27, 2024** and submitted it to
HCD for certification. No evidence found of any lapse in compliance since that date through
Sept 2026 — Millbrae did not appear on the Sept 2024 8-jurisdiction non-compliance roster
(Pacifica, Half Moon Bay, Daly City, San Bruno, Belmont, Atherton, Woodside, unincorporated
county) nor on the narrower late-2025 non-compliance list (San Mateo County unincorporated +
Half Moon Bay only).

HCD's statewide dashboard (https://www.hcd.ca.gov/planning-and-community-development/housing-open-data-tools/housing-element-implementation-and-apr-dashboard)
was checked but does not expose a per-jurisdiction status table in fetchable form — status above
is sourced from HCD's certification letter and the city's own certified-housing-element page
rather than the dashboard UI directly. Treat the dashboard citation as "consistent with" rather
than "directly confirms."

## SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Status

**SB9 (lot splits):** Millbrae has a **local implementing ordinance**, not bare state-default
process. Urgency Ordinance No. 793 (adopted **Dec 16, 2021**) established initial objective
standards for two-unit residential developments and urban lot splits under SB9. The City then
spent roughly a year in further review and adopted permanent **design standards** for SB9
proposals (Article XXXV — "Objective Standards and Affordability Requirements for Two-Unit
Residential Development and Urban Lot Splits") to promote visual/neighborhood consistency.
Characterized by local sources as "conservative but not excessively restrictive" — no unusual
height/FAR caps, no historic-district carve-out found, no owner-occupancy requirement found in
search results (UNCONFIRMED whether owner-occupancy affidavit is required — Burlingame's
ordinance has one; could not confirm Millbrae has an equivalent).

**SB35:** Millbrae/San Bruno were named in one source as qualifying for the SB35 10%-affordability
streamlining tier (insufficient progress toward Above Moderate Income RHNA). Because Millbrae is
now Housing-Element-compliant (as of March 2024), and SB35 eligibility is driven by an annual
RHNA-progress determination independent of Housing Element certification, Millbrae's status could
still be "subject to 10% tier" depending on the current-year HCD SB35 determination — **UNCONFIRMED**
for the Sept 2026 determination cycle specifically; no primary current-year HCD SB35 determination
table was retrieved. No SB35 project filings found for Millbrae in search results.

**SB10:** **No evidence found of Millbrae adopting a local SB10 resolution/ordinance** mapping
transit-rich or jobs-rich parcels near its BART/Caltrain intermodal station for up to 10
units/parcel. Search specifically probed for SB10 activity tied to the Millbrae
BART/Caltrain/SamTrans station area (including the completed "Gateway at Millbrae Station" BART
TOD project, opened 2023) and found no SB10 resolution — that project appears to have proceeded
under conventional TOD/specific-plan zoning, not an SB10 upzoning resolution. **Confirmed zero
adoption as best-available finding, not assumed** — but this is an absence-of-evidence finding,
not a direct negative confirmation from Millbrae's council records; a direct check of Millbrae
City Council resolution archives would be needed to fully rule out a resolution the search engines
did not surface.
