## Current Compliance Status (Housing Element)

**Compliant since April 25, 2024.** San Carlos's 2023-2031 (6th cycle) Housing Element was adopted
by the City Council on January 23, 2023, and certified by HCD on April 25, 2024, per San Carlos
Life (local outlet) coverage. RHNA allocation for the 6th cycle is 2,735 units across four income
categories.

This means San Carlos had a non-compliance window from the January 31, 2023 statutory deadline
until certification on April 25, 2024 — roughly 15 months — during which builder's-remedy exposure
existed in principle (see Builder's Remedy section below; no confirmed filings found).

Direct confirmation against HCD's live Housing Element Implementation and APR dashboard
(https://www.hcd.ca.gov/planning-and-community-development/housing-open-data-tools/housing-element-implementation-and-apr-dashboard)
was not independently re-verified this pass — status is sourced from local press (San Carlos Life)
rather than a direct HCD portal screenshot, so treat the exact certification date as
high-confidence-but-not-primary-source-verified.

## SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Status

**SB9 (lot splits):** San Carlos has its own **local implementing ordinance**, not pure state-default
handling. Timeline:
- January 10, 2022 — City Council adopted an urgency ordinance with interim objective standards for
  SB9 (urban lot split / two-unit) properties.
- November 13, 2023 — City Council adopted the **permanent SB9 Ordinance No. 1603**, effective
  December 13, 2023, codified at San Carlos Municipal Code §18.23.310 ("Urban Infill Unit"
  regulations).
- Local restrictions under the ordinance: SB9 units capped at 800 sq ft and 16 ft in height — the
  maximum restriction level state law permits. No discretionary review/public hearing is required
  for qualifying urban-infill projects (ministerial, per state law).
- No evidence found of an owner-occupancy requirement or historic-district carve-out specific to
  San Carlos; UNCONFIRMED whether such provisions exist in the full ordinance text (only summary
  sources were reviewed, not the full municipal code section).

**SB35:** No evidence found that San Carlos appears on HCD's SB35 streamlined-ministerial
determination list (10% or 50% affordability tier) for the current period. Given San Carlos reached
Housing Element compliance in April 2024, and SB35 eligibility criteria are tied to RHNA production
progress rather than solely Housing Element compliance status, a direct check of HCD's live SB35
determination dataset (data.ca.gov "Streamlined Ministerial Approval Process (SB35) Determination
Map") is recommended to confirm current tier, if any. No SB35 projects identified as filed or
approved in San Carlos in the sources reviewed.

**SB10:** No evidence found that San Carlos has adopted a local SB10 resolution/ordinance mapping
transit-rich or jobs-rich parcels for up to 10 units/parcel. Treating as **no adoption** — consistent
with the fact that no source surfaced any SB10-specific San Carlos action, and SB10 adoption is rare
statewide (voluntary, opt-in only). This is an absence-of-evidence inference, not a directly
confirmed negative record.
