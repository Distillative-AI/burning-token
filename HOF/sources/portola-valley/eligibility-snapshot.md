# Portola Valley — Builder's Remedy / SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Eligibility Snapshot (as of 2026-09-05)

## Current Compliance Status (Housing Element)

- Portola Valley's 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Housing Element was initially adopted/found in
  compliance around January 2024, but HCD **decertified** it on **March 26, 2024** — making
  Portola Valley the **first city or town in California** to have a previously-certified
  housing element revoked (later joined by Norwalk; Brisbane went through a related but
  distinct non-compliance/builder's-remedy episode). The stated basis was the Town's failure
  to implement required rezoning actions under Programs 1-1a, 1-1b, and 1-6 (the sites
  inventory rezone commitments).
- HCD sent a further correction letter on **October 16, 2024** indicating the Town's revised
  submittal still required additional tweaks (site analysis, program detail) before
  compliance could be restored.
- Throughout 2025 the Town pursued a "roadmap to a recertified housing element": exploring
  alternative sites (Village Square, Fogarty property, Christ Church) after the original
  Dorothy Ford Park high-density rezone (adopted by Council vote in **April 2025**) drew
  opposition; an Ad Hoc Site Evaluation Committee recommended substituting the Fogarty
  property (targeting ~50 units, discussed partly as farmworker housing) for some of the
  Dorothy Ford Park capacity.
- A Draft Housing Element Amendment was posted **December 23, 2025** for public review
  (through **January 14, 2026**); Town Council authorized submittal to HCD on
  **January 14, 2026**, and staff submitted it **January 23, 2026**.
- HCD returned comments in **March 2026**; the Town published a **second draft** amendment
  on **June 19, 2026** responding to those comments (added site analysis, a new program
  committing to publish a notice of funding availability with incentives for priority
  housing sites), and resubmitted to HCD on **July 16, 2026**.
- **As of September 2026, the Housing Element remains NOT YET RECERTIFIED** — the Town is
  mid-process on the amendment cycle, with additional public hearings expected in 2026
  before Council takes final action and HCD issues a compliance finding. Treat Portola
  Valley as **currently non-compliant / decertified**, pending HCD's response to the
  July 2026 resubmittal. **UNCONFIRMED**: exact HCD compliance determination date after the
  July 16, 2026 resubmittal — no source found confirming HCD has re-certified as of this
  writing.

## SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Status

**SB9 (lot splits / duplexes):**
- The Town has adopted a local implementing ordinance: **Portola Valley Municipal Code
  Chapter 17.13, "SB 9 Lot Splits,"** establishing objective standards for SB9 urban lot
  splits and two-unit developments on single-family zoned parcels, per Gov. Code §§65852.21
  and 66411.7. The ordinance is framed explicitly around preserving "rural character" and
  avoiding fire/geologic-safety and infrastructure impacts — consistent with the Town's
  large-lot (much of Portola Valley is zoned 1-acre-plus minimum), hillside, high Fire
  Hazard Severity Zone context.
- **UNCONFIRMED / not independently verified**: the full text of Chapter 17.13's specific
  numeric objective standards (minimum resulting lot size, setback figures, septic/well
  siting requirements, and whether the Town invokes SB9's statutory exemptions for
  properties in a state Fire Hazard Severity Zone or without adequate water/sewer/septic
  capacity). The Municode page for this chapter returned an access error during this
  research pass and could not be fetched; findings above are inferred from ordinance intent
  language found via search, not the operative section text itself. This should be
  independently re-verified against the live Municode/Town code before relying on it for
  a specific parcel determination.

**SB35 (streamlined ministerial approval):**
- No source found placing Portola Valley on HCD's SB35 determination list (neither the
  10%-affordability nor 50%-affordability tier). SB35 eligibility is generally restricted to
  jurisdictions in **urbanized areas/urban clusters** per Census designation; Portola Valley's
  low-density, largely unincorporated-feeling rural character makes non-applicability
  plausible, but **no HCD determination-summary excerpt specific to Portola Valley was
  retrieved** in this pass (the HCD SB35 Statewide Determination Summary PDF was located but
  not fetched for a line-item check). **UNCONFIRMED**: whether Portola Valley is formally
  listed as exempt/non-applicable, or simply absent because it falls outside urbanized-area
  scope. No SB35 project filings for Portola Valley were found in any source.

**SB10 (local upzoning resolution near transit/jobs-rich areas):**
- No evidence found that Portola Valley has adopted an SB10 resolution or ordinance mapping
  transit-rich or jobs-rich parcels. SB10 is opt-in via local legislative action, and nothing
  in Town Council agendas, news coverage, or the housing-element amendment process surfaced
  in this search references SB10 adoption. **Confirmed absence, to the extent searchable**:
  treat as **no SB10 adoption** in Portola Valley as of September 2026, though this is a
  negative finding (absence of evidence) rather than a directly-sourced Town statement of
  non-adoption — flagged accordingly below.

Portola Valley has been **exposed to builder's remedy** since its March 26, 2024
decertification (and remains exposed while non-compliant), and commentary from 2024-2025
coverage explicitly frames the Town as vulnerable to it. However, **no named project, unit
count, developer, or filing date was located** for an actual builder's remedy application
in Portola Valley specifically. This contrasts with peer decertified/non-compliant
jurisdictions (e.g., Brisbane, various Southern California cities) where specific builder's
remedy projects have been publicly reported. AB 1893 (effective 1/1/2025) changed the
statewide builder's remedy density-limit rules for any application filed on/after that date,
which is relevant background if a project surfaces later, but does not itself indicate a
Portola Valley filing.

**UNCONFIRMED**: absence of a builder's remedy project could reflect (a) genuinely no
applications filed, given the Town's difficult site conditions (septic/well, geologic
hazard, hillside fire zones may deter dense infill even under builder's remedy), or (b) a
gap in this research pass's source coverage (no direct query of San Mateo County/Town
planning department active-applications list or agenda packets was possible without site
access). Recommend a follow-up direct check of Town Council/Planning Commission agendas and
staff reports for 2024-2026 before concluding zero filings with high confidence.

## Recent Changes (2025-2026 ballot measures/ordinances)

- **April 2025**: Town Council voted to adopt the required rezoning of the Dorothy Ford Park
  site to high-density residential (part of the original Programs 1-1a/1-1b/1-6 compliance
  remedy), targeting roughly 50 homes on that site per the housing element inventory.
- **2025 (ongoing)**: Ad Hoc Site Evaluation Committee process explored substituting/adding
  the Fogarty property (~50 units, discussed in part as farmworker housing) and Village
  Square / Christ Church sites as alternative or supplemental rezone sites, partly in
  response to community pushback on Dorothy Ford Park.
- **December 23, 2025 – July 16, 2026**: Draft Housing Element Amendment cycle (draft
  posted, public comment, Council authorization to submit, HCD comments, second draft,
  resubmittal) — see Compliance section above for the date-by-date sequence.
- **No ballot measure or voter referendum** specific to Portola Valley housing/rezoning was
  found for 2025-2026. All changes identified were Town Council legislative/administrative
  actions (rezoning votes, housing element amendments), not ballot-box measures.
  **UNCONFIRMED**: cannot rule out a smaller local referendum petition effort not indexed by
  the search sources used; none was found.
