## Current Compliance Status (Housing Element)

- Foster City's 6th Cycle (2023–2031) Housing Element was adopted by City Council on
  **March 20, 2024** (Resolution No. 2024-17), following Planning Commission
  recommendation (Resolution P-2-24), after HCD found the January 31, 2024 draft
  revisions met statutory requirements on **February 2, 2024**.
- HCD issued full certification of the adopted Housing Element on **April 18, 2024**.
- As of this research (September 2026), Foster City's Housing Element is reported as
  **certified/compliant** and has been continuously since April 18, 2024 — no

## SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Status

**SB9 (lot splits):**
- Foster City's Housing Legislation page states the city follows SB9 as amended by
  **SB 450** (2023), which removed the ability to deny SB9 projects using
  project-specific objective standards (only underlying-zone standards apply) and
  imposed a 60-day deemed-approved timeline.
- No Foster City-specific implementing ordinance with additional local restrictions
  (e.g., owner-occupancy requirement, historic-district carve-out) was found in
  available sources. UNCONFIRMED whether Foster City has adopted a local objective
  standards ordinance beyond the state default — city's Housing Legislation page did
  not list one.

**SB35 (streamlined ministerial approval):**
- Foster City's Housing Legislation page notes SB35 was extended by **SB 423**
  (sunset moved from Jan 1, 2026 to Jan 1, 2036) and expanded by **SB 42**.
  Foster City does not itself set SB35 eligibility — that is determined annually by
  HCD's statewide SB35 determination list based on RHNA progress.
- Search results suggest Foster City appears on HCD's list of jurisdictions **not**
  currently subject to SB35's lower (10%) streamlining threshold as of recent
  determination cycles, implying it had been meeting above-moderate income RHNA
  progress — this is UNCONFIRMED at high confidence; the specific current-cycle
  (2025/2026) HCD SB35 Statewide Determination Summary PDF was not directly opened,
  only referenced in aggregate search snippets. Which tier (10% vs 50%) Foster City
  falls under for 2025-2026 is UNCONFIRMED and should be checked directly against the
  live HCD SB35 Statewide Determination Summary PDF.
- No specific SB35 project filings or approvals in Foster City were found in search
  results.

**SB10 (local upzoning resolution):**
- No evidence found that Foster City has adopted a local SB10 resolution/ordinance.
  Foster City's Housing Legislation page does not mention SB10 at all. Consistent
  with the general pattern that most California cities (including Foster City) have
  **not** adopted SB10 — CONFIRMED (as absence of any adoption record), though this is
  an absence-of-evidence finding rather than an explicit "City Council declined SB10"
  statement.
