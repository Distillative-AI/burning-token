# Half Moon Bay, CA — Builder's Remedy / SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Eligibility Snapshot

Research date: 2026-09-05. Status as of latest available filings (through ~April 2026 documents; no newer city/HCD filings found in search as of this research date).

## Current Compliance Status (Housing Element)

- Half Moon Bay never received timely 6th-cycle Housing Element certification. HCD issued a Failure-to-Adopt / Letter of Insufficiency dated **March 1, 2024** (`half-moon-bay-loi-030124.pdf`), consistent with the prior report's Sept 2024 non-compliance flag.
- City Council adopted a revised Housing Element on **November 4, 2025**.
- HCD responded **February 2, 2026**: the adopted Housing Element "met the statutory requirements of State Housing Law" on its face — but HCD explicitly stated it **cannot be found in "substantial compliance"** until the City completes required rezonings to cure the RHNA site-capacity shortfall (per Gov. Code §65585).
- **March 24, 2026**: HCD issued a Notice of Violation; Half Moon Bay was one of 15 CA jurisdictions listed as out of compliance with State Housing Element Law (tied to Gov. Newsom's "final warning" round to non-complying cities), and also flagged the City's ADU ordinance as non-compliant (response required by May 9, 2026).
- **April 20, 2026**: City submitted a response letter to HCD (Housing Accountability Unit, Chief Melinda Coy).
- **Net status as of this research (Sept 2026): NON-COMPLIANT / not in "substantial compliance."** The Housing Element text is deemed statutorily sufficient, but the City remains out of substantial compliance pending completion and HCD approval of required rezonings — meaning **Housing Accountability Act consequences, including Builder's Remedy exposure, remain in force** until rezonings are finalized. No source found confirming rezonings have since been completed/approved as of Sept 2026 — UNCONFIRMED whether status changed between April 2026 and Sept 2026.
- Complication unique to Half Moon Bay: **100% of the city is within the California Coastal Zone**, and the City has taken the position (per city officials/coastsidebuzz reporting) that **Builder's Remedy does not apply within the Coastal Zone**, an assertion contested by the state (per Governor/AG correspondence referenced in reporting) — **UNCONFIRMED/contested legal question, not judicially resolved as of Sept 2026** based on available sources.
- HCD's Housing Element Implementation/APR dashboard (https://www.hcd.ca.gov/planning-and-community-development/housing-open-data-tools/housing-element-implementation-and-apr-dashboard) was referenced as the canonical status source but was **not directly fetched/confirmed in this research** — treat the dashboard's live record as authoritative over this snapshot if it conflicts. **UNCONFIRMED** — dashboard not independently queried.

## SB9 / SB35 / SB10 Status

**SB9 (lot splits):**
- Because Half Moon Bay is entirely within the Coastal Zone, SB9 cannot take ministerial effect until the City adopts a local SB9 implementing ordinance and it is certified by the California Coastal Commission (this is a Coastal Act-driven requirement, not optional).
- City planning/community-development staff have publicly stated (per Coastside Buzz interview with the Community Development Director) how SB9 "will and will not work" in Half Moon Bay, but no source confirms the implementing ordinance has been Coastal-Commission-certified and is currently in force. Staff do not expect significant SB9 uptake regardless, citing small lot sizes and the cost of new water-service connections (water connection moratorium/capacity constraints are a known Coastside-specific constraint).
- **UNCONFIRMED**: exact adoption/certification date of a Half Moon Bay SB9 implementing ordinance, and whether it includes owner-occupancy or historic-district carve-outs. Not found in searches performed.

**SB35 (streamlined ministerial approval):**
- The City maintains a public SB35 process page (halfmoonbay.gov/1081) directing applicants to the ABAG SB35 application guide, confirming the City acknowledges SB35 applicability notwithstanding coastal-zone status.
- Active/filed project: **Metzgar & Main** (MidPen Housing / also reported with Mercy Housing attorney commentary — see Unconfirmed section for the developer-name discrepancy), 52 units, 100% affordable — exceeds SB35's affordability threshold, so it would qualify under the deeper affordability tier regardless of the City's 10%/50% HCD determination tier. Notice of Intent filed June 3, 2026; City Council held the mandatory SB35 public hearing June 16, 2026; Planning Commission received an informational SB35-process presentation June 23, 2026; objective design standards for the site were adopted by the City in December 2024; permit process targeted to begin February 2027.
- **UNCONFIRMED**: whether HCD's SB35 determination lists Half Moon Bay under the 10% or 50% affordability tier for market-rate-qualifying projects — the City's own determination-tier page/HCD determination list was not directly fetched in this research; the Metzgar & Main project sidesteps the question since it is 100% affordable.
- **UNCONFIRMED**: exact interaction between SB35 ministerial review and Coastal Development Permit requirements for this specific project (SB35 is understood generally to still require a CDP process in the Coastal Zone, per the City Attorney's public comments referenced in Coastside Buzz reporting, but no primary document was fetched confirming the specifics for this project).

**SB10:**
- No evidence found of Half Moon Bay adopting a local SB10 ordinance/resolution mapping transit-rich or jobs-rich parcels for up to 10 units/parcel. Searches of city planning pages and news returned no SB10-specific local action — **confirmed absence (zero adoption) is the best-supported conclusion**, consistent with the general statewide pattern of very low SB10 uptake. Note SB10's sunset for new ordinance adoption is January 1, 2029, so the City retains a future window.

Given the City's stated legal position that Builder's Remedy does not apply within its Coastal Zone jurisdiction, and no lawsuit or filed BR application was found in sources reviewed, **the most defensible current answer is zero confirmed Builder's Remedy project filings in Half Moon Bay as of Sept 2026** — but this is a negative result from search coverage, not a canonical registry check (e.g., California Housing Defense Fund's project tracker was not directly queried). **Flagged as UNCONFIRMED absence, not verified absence.**

## Recent Changes (2025-2026 ballot measures/ordinances)

- **Measure Q** (Nov. 3, 2026 ballot): Referendum on the City Council's approval of a 99-year ground lease of City-owned land at 535–555 Kelly Avenue to Mercy Housing California for a 40-unit affordable senior/farmworker housing project. Placed on the ballot after the "Let Half Moon Bay Voters Decide" group submitted 1,085 signatures. A "Yes" vote confirms/allows the project to proceed; a "No" vote blocks the lease.
- Council approved the underlying 555 Kelly ground-lease/phasing agreement around **December 4, 2025**.
- City adopted revised objective design standards (referenced in the Metzgar & Main SB35 process) in **December 2024** — predates the Sept 2026 research window but is directly load-bearing for the 2026 SB35 filing.
- City adopted a Housing Element update on **November 4, 2025** (see Compliance section) — the central 2025 ordinance-adjacent action affecting housing approvals citywide.
- ADU ordinance found non-compliant by HCD as of the March 24, 2026 notice; City given until May 9, 2026 to respond/update — **UNCONFIRMED** whether an amended ADU ordinance has since been adopted.
