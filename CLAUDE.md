This project follows /cfd (Constitution Art. I §7-9): durable cognition artifacts — research
findings, distilled fundamentals, policy logic — are authored and persisted as `.af.scm`/`.hof.scm`
citizens in the HOF chronology FIRST, verified to evaluate in the interpreter, and only then
pointed to from markdown. This file is intentionally a thin pointer, not the source of truth.

Search the chronology via `hof grep <query>` before raw `grep`/`rg`/`find`.

Research pipeline in use for this project: `/deep9` (retrieve-before-author kaizen over the
`/deep7` lanes), sourcing priority judgment via `/pareto-razor` (epistemic: which structural
cause is probably load-bearing) and `/pareto-prioritizer` (operational: rank findings/backlog by
impact-per-cost), and legal/regulatory research via `/paralegal`.
